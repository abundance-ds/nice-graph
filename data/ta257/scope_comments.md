|Population|Roche Products|The population should not only be “suitable for aromatase<br>inhibitors” but also ineligible for chemotherapy. As<br>suggested in the background section, the choice of either a<br>taxane or an aromatase inhibitor is dependent on whether a<br>patient is considered to require immediate chemotherapy. It<br>is therefore only after chemotherapy is deemed unsuitable<br>that the decision to treat with an aromatase inhibitor +/- a<br>biological is relevant.<br>If patients eligible for chemotherapy are included in the<br>scope, this will create an overlap with the ongoing re-review<br>of NICE TA34 and may inappropriately displace the recent<br>recommendations from CG81.|At the scoping workshop in June 2009, clinical<br>experts indicated that chemotherapy is not<br>necessarily unsuitable for this group of patients,<br>and may be a treatment option at a later stage<br>of the disease. This is also reflected in the NICE<br>Clinical Guideline on Advanced Breast Cancer<br>(CG81). Chemotherapy has not been included<br>as a comparator in this appraisal reflecting the<br>focus on women for whom endocrine therapy is<br>considered appropriate.|
