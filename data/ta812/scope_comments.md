## **Untreated** 

• Pembrolizumab, with pemetrexed and platinum chemotherapy (TA557) **Treated** • Docetaxel, with or without nintedanib Pembrolizumab, with pemetrexed and platinum chemotherapy is available regardless of PD-L1 status and is seen as the most effective and most commonly used treatment option in untreated patients.
