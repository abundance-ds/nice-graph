||Amgen|One of the outcomes listed in the draft scope is the reduction in symptoms of<br>which bleeding episodes is key. Given the differences in the methodology,<br>specification, definition and collection of bleeding events (pre-defined<br>secondary endpoint for eltrombopag trial versus post-hoc analysis of reported<br>safety data for romiplostim trial) across eltrombopag and romiplostim trials, it is<br>highly likely that any comparison performed on the relative risk of bleeding<br>events between eltrombopag and romiplostim is likely to be crude,<br>inappropriate and unreliable. For example, it will be inappropriate to compare<br>significant bleeding events defined as Grade 3-4 (severe, life-threatening or<br>fatal) for romiplostim with bleeding events defined as WHO Grades 2-4 for<br>eltrombopag as the latter definition also includes mild/moderate bleeding<br>events. Therefore, any such comparison is likely to be spurious and lead to<br>factually inaccurate claims and conclusions.|Comment noted.|
