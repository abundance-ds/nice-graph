||Pfizer|The draft scope words the population as:<br>“People with advanced hormone-receptor positive HER2-negative breast<br>cancer that has progressed after endocrine therapy.”<br>For this population, it is important to distinguish when patients have<br>progressed after endocrine therapy, i.e. at what stage progression occurred.<br><br>Patients who successfully complete endocrine therapy in the adjuvant<br>setting would be expected to be given endocrine therapy as a first-line<br>metastatic treatment option. If patients’ disease then progresses on a<br>first-line endocrine therapy in the metastatic setting, abemaciclib in<br>combination with fulvestrant may be an appropriate treatment (i.e. as<br>a second-line therapy in the metastatic setting). These patients are<br>thus relevant to this appraisal.<br><br>However, another population relevant for this appraisal are those who<br>did not successfully complete endocrine therapy in the adjuvant and<br>the neoadjuvant setting (i.e. disease advanced before the end of, or<br>soon after, adjuvant or neoadjuvant treatment), and then present for<br>first-line treatment in the metastatic setting. Due to the limited success<br>of adjuvant or neoadjuvant endocrine therapy in these patients,<br>abemaciclib plus fulvestrant could be considered as a first-line<br>treatment option in the metastatic setting for these patients.<br>The population is correctly worded in that abemaciclib plus fulvestrant is a<br>treatment option for disease which has progressed after endocrine therapy,<br>but it is important to note that this may include after first-line metastatic<br>endocrine therapy (i.e. as a second-line metastatic treatment option), but also<br>directly after adjuvant or neoadjuvant endocrine therapy (i.e. as a first-line<br>metastatic treatment option).|Comment noted. The<br>population has been<br>updated in the final<br>scope.|
