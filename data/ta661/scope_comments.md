## **Comment 2: the draft scope** 

|**Section**|**Consultee/**<br>**Commentator**|**Comments [sic]**|**Action**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Background<br>information|Merck, Sharp<br>and Dohme|No comments|Comment noted. No<br>action required.|
|The technology/<br>intervention|Merck, Sharp<br>and Dohme|We suggest revision to:<br>“Pembrolizumab within its marketing authorisation as monotherapy or in<br>combination with platinum and 5-fluorouracil chemotherapy”|Comment noted.<br>‘Platinum-based’ is a<br>broad term which is<br>intended to also cover<br>other therapies used<br>alongside platinum<br>chemotherapies. |Population|Merck, Sharp<br>and Dohme|No comments|Comment noted. No<br>action required.|
|Comparators|Merck, Sharp<br>and Dohme|No comments|Comment noted. No<br>action required.|
|Outcomes|Merck, Sharp<br>and Dohme|We agree with the proposed outcome measures.<br>In addition, we suggest inclusion of ‘duration of response’ as an additional<br>outcome measure. It is known that the response to immunotherapies<br>(immuno-oncology drugs) may be delayed, but once triggered, is likely to be<br>durable, bringing long-term survival benefit for a subset of patients; this<br>benefit is not captured by the proposed outcome measures, but would be<br>captured in the duration of response outcome.|Comment noted. No<br>change to the scope is<br>necessary as duration<br>of response would be<br>covered by ‘response<br>rate’. The list of<br>outcomes is not<br>intended to be<br>exhaustive.|
