|||**The scope currently covers the population from TA540 that is eligible**<br>**for treatment through the Cancer Drugs Fund, i.e. adults with relapsed**<br>**or refractory classical Hodgkin lymphoma who have had brentuximab**<br>**vedotin and cannot have autologous stem cell transplant (ASCT). Is this**<br>**population appropriate, or do you consider that a full review of TA540 is**<br>**needed, to include adults who have had both AHSCT and brentuximab**<br>**vedotin?**<br>MSD response: MSD considers that the population listed in the draft scope is<br>appropriate and believes it unnecessary to review again the evidence for the<br>cohort of people with R/RcHL who have had autologous SCT and<br>brentuximab vedotin, a population for which NICE previously did not<br>recommend pembrolizumab.<br>**Which treatments are considered to be established clinical practice in**<br>**the NHS for people with relapsed or refractory classical Hodgkin**<br>**lymphoma who have had a ASCT or for whom ASCT is not suitable?**<br>MSD response: Based on NICE recommendations, and as detailed in the<br>background information, established practice involves use of brentuximab<br>vedotin and pembrolizumab for those with R/RcHL who have failed<br>autologous SCT, or for whom autologous SCT is not suitable and who have<br>received at least two prior lines of therapy. Additionally, for those with R/RcHL<br>who have undergone autologous SCT and treatment with brentuximab<br>vedotin, in any order, nivolumab is also an option. However, there is no<br>standard of care for those who fail treatment with brentuximab vedotin without<br>undergoing autologous SCT, or those who fail on pembrolizumab, either after<br>autologous SCT or systemic treatment alone.||
