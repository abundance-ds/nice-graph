|||Endometrial cancer is a cancer of the lining of the womb (uterus), known as<br>the endometrium. It is the most common type of womb cancer, often<br>diagnosed in the earlier stages. When diagnosed, endometrial cancer is<br>categorised between stage 1 and 4. Advanced endometrial cancer is defined<br>as stage 3 or 4, where the cancer has spread outside the womb.
