|Kidney<br>Research UK|To our knowledge, there is no direct evidence to suggest that<br>dapagliflozin has equal efficacy to empagliflozin in the population<br>suggested (eGFR 20 - 90 ml/min) for this appraisal. However, both<br>drugs belong to the same class of drugs and have similar mechanism<br>of action. A recent target trial emulation study has demonstrated that<br>the use of SGLT2i (including both drugs) in those with eGFR <20<br>ml/min is associated with lower risk of dialysis, hospitalisation for heart<br>failure and AKI (Yen F, et al. Annals of Internal Medicine, 2024). It is<br>likely that dapagliflozin is equally effective/ beneficial and safe as<br>empagliflozin in the suggested population.|Thank you for your<br>comment. No action<br>required.|
