|Innovation|MSD|Yes, we would consider the technologies peginterferon alfa-2a and -2b to be<br>innovative.  Successful treatment of chronic hepatitis C can prevent children<br>from going on to develop liver damage and cirrhosis, thus avoiding the<br>potential consequences of hepatocellular carcinoma and the need for liver<br>transplant.  Peginterferon alfa-2a and -2b are also dosed once weekly,<br>compared to the thrice weekly dosing of interferon alfa, thus reducing the<br>burden of the treatment for patients.  The dosing of peginterferon alfa-2b is<br>based on patients’ body surface area. Viraferonpeg is available in pens of<br>different strengths, and the dose administered by each pen can be altered.<br>This allows flexibility, in particular if the patient's dose needs altering.|Comment noted.|
