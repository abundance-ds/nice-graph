The results<br>of the completed randomised phase 3 studies VIALE-C (LDAC+/- VEN) and<br>VIALE-A (AZA +/- VEN) have both been reported/published this year. VIALE-<br>C confirmed the high response rates with the addition of Venetoclax but did<br>not meet the predetermined primary endpoint in terms of improved overall<br>survival.
