The serum levels must be_<br>_controlled due to a very narrow therapeutic range, in which most therapeutic_<br>_benefit occurs only when near-toxic doses are given (GOLD 2015a). The use_<br>_of theophylline is limited by the frequency of adverse effects._<br>In addition, the previous roflumilast appraisal investigated the ability to<br>conduct a trial comparing roflumilast to theophylline and the TAG states “the<br>Committee noted comments received during consultation, and agreed that<br>such a trial could be difficult to recruit to because of contraindications, side<br>effects, and additional monitoring needed for people on theophylline.
