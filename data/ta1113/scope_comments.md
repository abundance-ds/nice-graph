|Timing Issues|Roche|For patients who are ineligible for autologous stem cell transplant (ASCT), the<br>most widely used regimens for patients after first relapse of DLBCL are<br>gemcitabine and/or platinum-based chemotherapy with or without rituximab<br>(e.g. R-GemOx). Unfortunately, the majority of patients will be refractory or<br>relapse after these second line combinations and will require further lines of<br>treatment.<br>The CAR-T cell therapy, axicabtagene ciloleucel, has recently been approved<br>for funding via the CDF for patients who are refractory to, or who have<br>relapsed within 12 months of first line chemoimmunotherapy and are suitable<br>for ASCT (TA895).<br>Combining glofitamab with gemcitabine plus oxaliplatin is anticipated to<br>provide improved response rates, which may translate into improved OS,<br>compared with the combination of rituximab and GemOx, while maintaining an<br>acceptable safety profile in the second line treatment of patients who are not<br>suitable for ASCT.<br>There have been several recent approvals of treatments for third and<br>subsequent line treatment of DLBCL, including the CD20xCD3 bispecific<br>antibodies, glofitamab and epcoritamab, as monotherapy (TA927, TA954).
