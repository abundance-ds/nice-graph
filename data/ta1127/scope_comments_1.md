Clinical advice was that, for<br>this small group of patients, CRT has been displaced by neoadjuvant<br>nivolumab with chemotherapy.5<br>▪<br>The committee agreed it is not appropriate to compare outcomes from<br>studies evaluating adjuvant treatments with outcomes from studies<br>including perioperative or neoadjuvant treatments because the<br>populations, and timepoints for decisions, differ.6Specifically, studies<br>reviewing adjuvant treatments include patients who have undergone<br>resection, whilst studies reviewing perioperative and neoadjuvant<br>treatments include patients who have not yet undergone resection.<br>`o`<br>Therefore, adjuvant chemotherapy, adjuvant osimertinib, and<br>atezolizumab after adjuvant cisplatin-based chemotherapy are not<br>considered relevant to this appraisal.|Thank you for your<br>comment.
