|Innovation|Celgene|Lenalidomide in combination with rituximab represents a<br>chemotherapy-free approach to the treatment of relapsed/refractory<br>follicular and marginal zone lymphoma, which remains an area of high<br>unmet medical need with limited treatment options. The only currently<br>reimbursed treatment alternatives in England and Wales are<br>chemotherapy-based, and the combination of lenalidomide and<br>rituximab represents a targeted, synergistic, immunomodulatory<br>approach that is a step change away from chemotherapy, offering a<br>different class of treatment. The addition of lenalidomide to rituximab<br>results in a complimentary mechanism of action in indolent NHL, that<br>causes enhanced immunomodulatory and direct anti-tumour activity<br>compared to either agent used alone, as a result of increased natural<br>killer cell function, increased immune synapse formation, increased<br>apoptosis and increased antibody-dependent cell-mediated<br>cytotoxicity. Additionally, the combination of lenalidomide and<br>rituximab may reduce administrative burden compared to rituximab<br>plus chemotherapy for both the patient and the NHS, as lenalidomide<br>is an oral therapy.1-10|Thank you, your comments<br>have been noted. No changes<br>have been made.|
