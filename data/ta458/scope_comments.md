|Innovation|Breakthrough<br>Breast Cancer|T-DM1 has a duel method of action. By attaching to the HER2 receptor on the<br>surface of the cancer cell it blocks the signals that encourages the cancer to<br>grow and spread. It then becomes internalised by the cell where it releases a<br>cytotoxic agent which destroys the cell from the inside.<br>This targeted approach means healthy cells are left untouched and the<br>common side effects of chemotherapy are reduced.<br>Therefore, the method of action and the subsequent impact on chemotherapy<br>related side effects of this treatment can indeed be classed as innovative.|Comments noted.|
