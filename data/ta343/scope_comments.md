||Roche Products|Obinutuzumab is innovative in two distinct ways; being a first-in-class Type II<br>anti-CD20 antibody, and being glycoengineered.<br>Obinutuzumab is expected to be the first Type II CD20 antibody to be<br>licensed for CD20+ve malignancies. In contrast to Type I antibodies, Type II<br>antibodies not only induce CDC and ADCC, but also potently induce direct<br>cell death, and as such induce targeted, anti-leukaemic effects on<br>malignancies such as CLL.<br>Furthermore, through glycoengineering, obinutuzumab potently induces<br>antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC), being enhanced by up to<br>100-fold compared with type I antibodies such as rituximab. ADCC is thought<br>to play an important role in anti-leukaemic effect of treatments for CLL.<br>These distinct, innovative characteristics of obinutuzumab are thought to<br>strongly contribute to the results seen in the CLL11 trial.
