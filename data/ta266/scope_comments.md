||Pharmaxis|What is Bronchitol?<br>Bronchitol has been subject to a 10 year pharmaceutical, preclinical and<br>clinical development program and is covered by patents granted in most major<br>jurisdictions.  Bronchitol is precision spray dried mannitol manufactured to form<br>hollow spheres with a mean particle size of 3 microns, encapsulated in gelatine<br>capsules and delivered via an easy to use dry powder inhaler.  The production<br>process developed by Pharmaxis is carefully controlled to deliver a narrow<br>range of particle size.
