Our reasoning for this recommendation<br>is explained below.<br>An independent review and network meta-analysis conducted by Samjoo et<br>al. in 2023 showed that ublituximab is as clinically effective as other NICE-<br>recommended mAb therapies, such as ofatumumab, natalizumab,<br>alemtuzumab, and ocrelizumab. Among these,<br>alemtuzumab, ofatumumab, and ublituximab exhibit the most pronounced<br>efficacy compared to placebo, with relative risks ranging from 0.28 to 0.31.<br>Similarly, in terms of 3mCDP and 6mCDP, these mAb therapies, along with<br>ponesimod and cladribine, emerge as the most effective options compared to<br>placebo, as indicated by Hazard Ratios ranging from 0.39 to 0.59 and 0.41 to<br>0.54, respectively.<br>Our recent (currently unpublished) systematic review and network meta-<br>analysis confirm the findings of Samjoo et al. in 2023, reinforcing the strength<br>of these conclusions. This evidence<br>underscores the potential benefits of ublituximab for patients with RMS, a<br>group for which teriflunomide is currently recommended.||
