|Innovation|Amgen|**_Do you consider the technology to be innovative in its potential to make_**<br>**_a significant and substantial impact on health-related benefits and how_**<br>**_it might improve the way that current need is met (is this a ‘step-change’_**<br>**_in the management of the condition)?_**<br>We believe that due to its novel, targeted mechanism of action, evolocumab<br>offers a significant medical advance with an associated step-change in the<br>treatment of patients with hypercholesterolaemia and mixed dyslipidaemia<br>who are at increased risk of CV events.<br>Evolocumab is a fully human monoclonal immunoglobulin G2 (IgG2) antibody<br>that binds with high affinity and specificity to human proprotein convertase<br>subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9), a protein that plays an essential role in lipid<br>metabolism by recycling and regulating hepatic cell-surface LDL receptors<br>(LDLR).
