These two antibodies<br>are from the same original hybridoma clone, and have<br>identical amino acid sequences, but have been grown in<br>different producer cell lines (SP2/0 and CHO<br>respectively). There are no clincal studies directly<br>comparing the two agents, but as they are grown in<br>different cell lines they are likely to have different<br>glycosylation patterns which might signifiantly affect<br>effector function. ch14.18 SP/20 (Dinutuximab) has been<br>used in the North American Children's Oncology Group<br>(COG) clinical trials, and ch14.18 /CHO has been used in<br>the several European SIOPEN trials.<br>In view of the potential functional differences between<br>these agents, it should not be assumed that the clinical<br>effects are the same, or that the benefit (if any) of<br>combining antibody with cytokines (e.g. IL-2 and GM-CM-<br>CSF) is equivalent.<br>NICE has already undertaken a technology appraisal for<br>Dinutuximab and it will be important for APN311 to be<br>considered in this context, whilst recognising that there<br>may be biological differences between these agents.||
