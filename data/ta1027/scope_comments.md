|Innovation|Immunocore|An application for Tebentafusp has been submitted to the MHRA for the<br>Innovative Licensing and Access Pathway (application number<br>ILAP/IP/21/36781/01).<br>Tebentafusp is a highly targeted immunotherapy which acts in a completely<br>new way. It is from a new class of treatment – bispecific soluble T-cell<br>receptor therapeutics, termed ImmTACs (Immune mobilising monoclonal T-<br>cell receptors Against Cancer), that are designed to activate a patient’s own<br>T-cells against specific cancer cells.<br>Tebentafusp is the first ImmTAC based therapy to show survival benefit in a<br>randomised trial, the first bispecific drug to show a survival benefit in a solid<br>tumour and the first therapy to show a significant survival benefit in metastatic<br>uveal melanoma.10<br>Tebentafusp has been developed specifically for the treatment of the<br>metastatic form of this ultra-rare cancer, which affects around 540 patients in<br>the UK.<br>Tebentafusp will set a new standard of care offering the first effective<br>treatment option for HLA-A*02:01-positive patients with unresectable or<br>metastatic disease. Tebentafusp is designed to recognise and direct the<br>body’s immune system to destroy uveal melanoma cells that express a gp100<br>peptide presented by HLA-A*02:01. Hence, immune activation by tebentafusp<br>is specific to uveal melanoma cells.<br>**Benefits outside of the QALY**<br>By providing patients and clinicians with the first treatment specifically<br>licensed for metastatic uveal melanoma, tebentafusp has the potential to<br>catalyse improvement in the treatment pathway, by providing an effective<br>treatment following early detection of metastatic disease.|Thank you for your<br>comment. No action<br>needed.|
