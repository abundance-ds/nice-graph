## **Olaparib with abiraterone for untreated hormone-relapsed metastatic prostate cancer [ID3920] Response to stakeholder organisation comments on the draft remit and draft scope** 

**Please note:** Comments received in the course of consultations carried out by NICE are published in the interests of openness and transparency, and to promote understanding of how recommendations are developed.  The comments are published as a record of the submissions that NICE has received, and are not endorsed by NICE, its officers or advisory committees. 

**Comment 1: the draft remit and proposed process** 

|**Section**|**Stakeholder**|**Comments [sic]**|**Action**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Appropriateness<br>of an evaluation<br>and proposed<br>evaluation route|Prostate Cancer<br>UK|Prostate cancer UK welcomes the evaluation of olaparib used in conjunction<br>with abiraterone for untreated, metastatic hormone resistant prostate cancer.<br>There are several treatments available in the metastatic castrate resistant<br>setting, such as docetaxel, abiraterone, enzalutamide, or cabazitaxel. Radium<br>223 is a further last line treatment. There are currently no precision<br>treatments available for prostate cancer.<br>However, there still remains a strong need for further treatments in this<br>indication that offer good clinical benefit and improvement in progression free<br>survival and it is clear from the evidence in the PROpel trial that this drug<br>combination could fit this remit.<br>Should the proposed appraisal recommend that abiraterone with olaparib is<br>effective for the above indication, it will help to provide and improve<br>standardised access and increased treatment choice to a group of patients<br>who currently have a restricted range of treatments available. We also believe<br>that the single technology appraisal route is appropriate in this instance.|Thank you for your<br>comment. No action<br>required.|
