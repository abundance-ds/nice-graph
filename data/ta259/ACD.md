FASTRAC is a trial which exploits the abiraterone<br>food effect. Abiraterone has the most marked food effect of any drug in medicine.<br>Bioavailability is increased five to ten fold if the drug is given with a meal.  Yet the<br>manufacturers recommend that four tablets (1000mg) be taken on an empty<br>stomach. In FASTRAC we propose restricting abiraterone to a cost effectiveness<br>trial, in which one tablet taken immediately after a meal is compared to four tablets<br>taken on an empty stomach.  This trial would save over £19million in drug costs<br>and would be a less costly route to providing access to abiraterone.<br>One option for the NICE panel would be to liaise with the HTA and recommend<br>abiraterone only within the FASTRAC trial.  This would link the research and policy<br>arms of the NHS for the first time and provide a new approach to the provision of<br>costly new cancer drugs.  The full HTA FASTRAC protocol is available on request<br>fromtheProstate CSG Chair.|
