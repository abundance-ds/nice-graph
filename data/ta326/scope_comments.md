||Sarcoma UK|Understanding overall survival (OS) as an outcome measure presents<br>challenges because of the successive therapies now available to patients. A<br>patient relapsing following adjuvant imatinib will receive imatinib as first-line<br>therapy. The study needs to be aware that patients on imatinib post-recurrence<br>can continue on treatment for many years (currently there are patients at 12+<br>years). Physical decline due to the disease follows resistance to imatinib and<br>later therapies are not as effective in offering control. Once imatinib resistance<br>occurs interventions can include surgery, RFA, second- and third- line TKI<br>therapies, and use of new radiotherapy technology. There is also an active<br>research programme internationally and new therapies are in trials.<br>The EORTC trial used the principle outcome of ‘resistance to first TKI’<br>attempting to identify a point in the pathway of disease which could not be<br>distorted by later therapies and which is regarded as a determinant of survival.<br>The SSG study reported recurrence free survival (RFS) as its primary outcome<br>and OS as a secondary endpoint . All patients received imatinib therapy (a two-<br>arm randomisation between 1 and 3 years). It reported an improvement in both<br>RFS and OS for patients taking adjuvant therapy for 3 years over those who<br>took it for one year.<br>The ACOSOG study was placebo controlled and used recurrence-free survival<br>as its principle outcome measure.<br>We recognise the value of the OS endpoint but because of the clear benefit<br>imatinib offers in first-line therapy we believe that rather than recurrence-free<br>survival (taking the first recurrence as the endpoint) the endpoint adopted by<br>EORTC (resistance to first TKI) should be considered as the secondary<br>outcome measure for this Appraisal. It offers a fairer view of the benefits of<br>adjuvant therapy because it records the most important prognostic<br>development in the patient pathway.|Comments noted. Both overall<br>survival and recurrence-free<br>survival are outcomes listed in<br>the scope. No change to the<br>scope required.|
