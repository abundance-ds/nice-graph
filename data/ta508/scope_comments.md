|The technology/<br>intervention|Co.don AG|In the product chondrosphere®, similar as to other MACI products, the<br>chondrocytes are embedded in a three-dimensional scaffold resulting in an<br>equal distribution of the cells resulting in improved proliferation,<br>differentiation, migration and attachment of the cells compared to 1stACI<br>generation products.<br>However, chondrosphere®can be considered as a new generation compared<br>to the other MACI products, as no xenogenous scaffold is used as in the<br>other MACI products, but it contains an extra-cellular matrix produced by the<br>cells themselves.<br>Due to the 3D cell culture system used to produce chondrosphere®, the cells<br>aggregate, condense and develop cell-cell and cell-matrix contacts. This<br>results in the enhanced differentiation of the cells and production of a hyaline-<br>like extra-cellular matrix. The product chondrosphere®therefore contains cells<br>that are already in a chondrogenic state and started to produce extra-cellular<br>matrix, which they continue to produce upon implantation into the cartilage<br>defect.<br>Moreover, chondrosphere®is a pure autologous product and xenogenous<br>substances are completely absent, which reduces the risk of infections,<br>allergic reactions and rejections compared to other MACI products that do<br>contain a xenogenous scaffold. In addition, for implantation of<br>chondrosphere®also no xenogenous substances as fibrin glue or suturing are<br>required as for implantation of other MACI products, as chondrosphere®<br>adheres itself to the defect bottom after implantation.<br>Our clinical trials Phase II and III are currently ongoing. Results of the 4 years<br>follow up will be expected in March 2017. Due to a prolonged patient|Comment noted. No<br>action required.|
