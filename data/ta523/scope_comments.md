|Timing Issues|Leukaemia<br>CARE|Acute myeloid leukaemia (AML) makes up about 33% of all leukaemia cases<br>in the UK and is fatal if not treated. Prognosis following an AML diagnosis is<br>poor, particularly in older adults. On average, 27% of those diagnosed will<br>survive for five years or more after they have received treatment. This drops<br>to just 5% survival for patients who over the age of 65.<br>Because it is heterogeneous, AML is a disease that is unlikely to respond in a<br>consistent pattern to non-targeted therapy. The existing cytarabine based<br>regimens used to treat AML demonstrate limited efficacy. Patients that<br>present the FLT3 mutation are associated with a particularly poor prognosis<br>and there are limited treatment options for AML that specifically target the<br>mutation. As such, thereis anurgentneedfor new, effective treatment|Comment noted. No<br>action required.|

|Innovation|Leukaemia<br>CARE|In a disease area where there is a poor prognosis, there has been little<br>advancement in treatment in the last few decades.<br>Patients in this setting (with the FLT3 mutation) are more likely to relapse<br>after receiving this treatment and are considered high risk AML patients. In a<br>setting where initial treatment is unlikely to be successful due to this particular<br>mutation, midostaurin represents an effective, tolerable treatment option that<br>offers a deepened response and survival benefit (when used in combination<br>with induction therapy).<br>Furthermore, it has shown potential to bridge some patients to transplant (the<br>only curative treatment option for AML). For patients with FLT3 mutations,<br>transplant would currently be an unlikely treatment option, as the desired<br>response needed for a transplant is difficult to reach using existing treatment<br>options.<br>As such, midostaurin can be considered an innovative treatment option.|Comment noted.<br>The innovative nature of<br>midostaurin will be<br>taken into account in<br>the committee’s<br>discussion.<br>No action required.|
