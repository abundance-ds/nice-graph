||NCRI-ACP-<br>RCP-RCR|Tivozanib is an oral VEGF inhibitor used in the setting of mRCC. Whilst data<br>is awaited from ongoing clinical studies, previous studies have demonstrated<br>that it has efficacy and a manageable toxicity profile in this setting. Several<br>other oral VEGF inhibitors (pazopanib, sunitinib, axitinib) are already<br>established in clinical practice in this setting in England. Therefore, whether<br>tivozanib represents a sufficient step change in terms of favourable efficacy<br>and toxicity profile to make a substantial impact on health-related benefits is<br>considered uncertain.|Comments noted.
