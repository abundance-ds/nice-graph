|Other<br>considerations|Gedeon Richter<br>(UK)|Patients with fibroids < 2cm in diameter were excluded from the Relugolix CT<br>clinical trials. |Innovation|Gedeon Richter<br>(UK)|There is a significant unmet patient need for a non-surgical treatment option<br>for the long-term management of the multiple symptoms associated with<br>uterine fibroids.<br>Relugolix CT is the first once-daily oral GnRH antagonist combined with add-<br>back therapy to be licensed in Europe for treatment of uterine fibroid<br>symptoms. Relugolix CT is a novel combination of active ingredients, with<br>relugolix blocking GnRH from binding to GnRH receptors in the anterior<br>pituitary, which results in suppressed secretion of FSH and LH. The resulting<br>decrease in endogenous production of E2 and progesterone caused by a<br>reduction in follicular growth and ovulation inhibits the growth and symptoms<br>of uterine fibroids. The addition of exogenous E2 leads to systemic oestrogen<br>concentrations consistent with those in the early follicular phase of the<br>menstrual cycle, which enables minimisation of bone mineral density loss and<br>vasomotor symptoms. The addition of NETA serves to prevent endometrial<br>hyperplasia.<br>NICE guidance supports shared decision making between health<br>professionals and patients, and we believe Relugolix CT offers patients<br>suffering with symptoms of uterine fibroids a long-term pharmacological<br>treatment option not currently available to them.|Thank you for your<br>comments. The<br>appraisal committee will<br>consider the innovative<br>nature of this<br>technology during the<br>appraisal. No action<br>required.|
