|Appropriateness|Celgene Ltd|We believe that it would be inappriopriate for this topic to be<br>appraised by NICE using the Single Technology Appraisal<br>(STA) process.  The remit for this proposed appraisal is for<br>multiple products (bortezomib and thalidomide), while the<br>STA process is designed by definition for the appraisal of a<br>single technology.  Specifically, the NICE Guide to the STA<br>process states that "The STA process is specifically<br>designed for the appraisal of a single product, device or<br>other technology, with a single indication, where most of the<br>relevant evidence lies with one manufacturer or sponsor."<br>Since the remit of this appraisal is for multiple products<br>(bortezomib and thalidomide) the appropriate process for<br>this appraisal is the Multuple Technology Appraisal (MTA)<br>process. The Glossary of NICE's Guide to the Topic<br>Selection Process Interim process manual states that<br>NICE’s multiple technology appraisal (MTA) process<br>focuses on evaluating evidence to determine whether<br>groups of drugs, devices or health technologies used to<br>treat a disease are clinically and cost effective. It is typically<br>used for a group of products, some of which may already be<br>in use within the NHS.|Comment noted. NICE has received referral of<br>this topic as a multiple technology appraisal.|
