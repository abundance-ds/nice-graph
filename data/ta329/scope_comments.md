||British Society of<br>Gastroenterology|The epidemiological aspects are correct. The background includes aspects of<br>the impact of ulcerative colitis on schooling, work relationships and quality of<br>life in general; such aspects should be considered when looking at treatment<br>outcomes, albeit that the published data may simply be ‘quality of life’.<br>The disease being reviewed should be better defined. The MTA refers to<br>moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis; the definitions of disease<br>activity (page 1) moderately active UL adequately, but severe disease (as<br>stated) defines acute severe disease: ie. patients who should be treated as in<br>patients. There is an inconsistency between the patients to be address by this<br>MTA and the BSG definitions.<br>The RCTs that underpin the product licenses are based on patients whose<br>disease activity was defined using the Mayo scoring tool. This tool calls<br>patients severe without the need for systemic features. Thus ‘Mayo-severe’ is<br>not the same as ‘BSG-severe’.<br>A better definition of the sick out-patient with UC is needed, that might define<br>a ‘severe’ outpatient in a similar way to the Mayo score (but without the need<br>to perform sigmoidoscopy.<br>A compromise might be to define moderate activity as per the BSG, a ‘non-<br>acute severe’ as those patients having more than bloody bowel movements<br>per day, but without tachycardia or fever. (Anaemia is not particularly useful<br>in assessing severity in outpatients, unless it of new on-set).<br>When managing outpatients, azathioprine / methotrexate are used as steroid<br>sparing agents; they are no used to induce remission.|Comment noted. Following<br>discussion at the scoping<br>workshop, the background<br>section of the scope has been<br>amended to clarify the<br>population under<br>consideration and the<br>Truelove and Witts severity<br>index has also been included.<br>Broadly, people with acute<br>severe ulcerative colitis with<br>tachycardic and febrile<br>symptoms are not included in<br>the scope of this appraisal as<br>guidance for this population is<br>covered by TA163 and<br>CG166.|
