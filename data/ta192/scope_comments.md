||AstraZeneca|Gefitinib is a potent and selective inhibitor of the epidermal growth factor<br>receptor (EGFR) tyrosine kinase. Activation of the tyrosine kinase catalyses<br>autophosphorylation and subsequent phosphorylation of protein tyrosine<br>residues, which then initiates a cellular signal transduction cascade. Selective<br>inhibition of the EGFR tyrosine kinase with gefitinib results in interruption of<br>mitogenic and anti-apoptotic signals responsible for cellular cancer processes<br>such as proliferation, growth, metastases, angiogenesis and responsiveness to<br>chemotherapy or radiotherapy. There is now considerable evidence of<br>expression and over-expression of EGFR in an extensive range of human<br>cancers eg, non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) as well as prostate, colorectal,<br>head and neck, bladder, breast and gastric cancers. Importantly, over-<br>expression of EGFR has been correlated with poor prognosis features in many<br>cases. In recent studies the efficacy of gefitinib has been clearly correlated to<br>the mutation status for the EGFR gene with EGFR mutation positive patients<br>benefiting most from gefitinib treatment."|Comment noted.
