|Background<br>information|Lymphoma<br>Association|Staging of advanced follicular lymphoma can be more complicated than<br>the draft scope suggests. Some people will have what appears to be<br>stage II disease but may have other test results suggesting the need for<br>treatment as advanced disease. Early stage disease is relatively rare.<br>Most patients have advanced disease at the time of diagnosis.<br>The section discussing treatment could do with a shift in emphasis and<br>some clarification. The aim of treatment for advanced disease is to<br>achieve the best quality remission possible, for as long as possible, with<br>as good a quality of life as possible. Prolonging survival is a clearly a<br>desirable outcome, but perhaps not the first thing on the list of treatment<br>objectives.<br>First line treatment options for advanced disease are: watchful waiting,<br>immuno-chemotherapy (R-chemo) or oral single agent chemotherapy for<br>people who cannot tolerate intravenous chemotherapy regimens. Some<br>patients will be ineligible for treatment with rituximab, in which case they<br>will be offered combination therapy without rituximab. Currently used<br>regimens include steroids.|Comments noted. The draft<br>scope has been updated.<br>Please note the scoping<br>document is only intended<br>to provide a brief summary<br>of the condition and its<br>management.|

||Royal College<br>Pathologists|The description of the technology is accurate. R-CVP is recommended<br>in NICE guidance TA110 as a front line treatment (Marcus, Blood 2005).<br>The rituximab UK marketing authorisation for this indication has now<br>been extended from R-CVP to rituximab in combination with any<br>chemotherapy regimen. This application seeks to extend the NICE<br>guidance accordingly. There are no details of possible alternative<br>chemotherapy regimens used in combination with rituximab. The<br>addition of rituximab to chemotherapy has shown to be beneficial in<br>terms of response rate and progression free survival in a number of<br>phase III studies. Other chemotherapy regimens used in these studies<br>include CHOP (cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine and<br>prednisolone), FCM (fludarabine, cyclophosphamide, mitozantrone), FM<br>(fludarabine and mitozantrone) (Hiddenmann Blood 2005, Forstpointner,<br>Blood 2004, Zinzani,J Clin Oncol 2004). Rituximab in combination with<br>single chemotherapy agents (chlorambucil, bendamustine or<br>fludarabine) might be appropriate for older individuals with co-<br>morbidities although phase III data is often lacking.<br>The most appropriate first-line treatment for follicular lymphoma patients<br>should be risk adapted and individualised taking into account co-<br>morbidities. Extending the NICE guidance for front line treatment of<br>follicular lymphoma to allow other R-chemotherapy combinations will<br>allow physicians to use their clinical acumen and evidence based<br>approach to treatment where possible.|Comment noted.|
