|**Section 1**<br>(Appraisal Committee's<br>preliminary<br>recommendations)|I am the Lead Consultant for Lancashire Care Early Intervention<br>Service (14 -35 yrs), with 3 adult consultant psychiatrists and on<br>CAMHS consultant psychiatrist. We also have seperate<br>inpatient units for 14-16 yrs old and 16-18 yrs old.<br>In my view clinical scale are useful to quantify outcomes but<br>impact on quality of life and social functioning are more<br>important.<br>Aripiprazole is one of the 4 most commonly prescribed<br>antipsychotics to clients in our service.<br>Data on tolerability is extremely important and aripiprazole has<br>the edge having lower propensity for weight gain and sexual<br>dysfunction.|
|**Section 2**<br>(the technology)|"I generally use smaller doses of aripiprazole, starting 5ms on<br>alternate days (it has a long half life) for 1-2 weeks, then 5 mgs<br>daili and then according to clinical response.<br>The liquid form is an advantage for smaller doses."|
|**Section 3**<br>(manufacturer's<br>submission)|"The data submitted by manufacturers of the drug is required<br>but for me most important is my/our own experience.<br>We find aripiprazole to be clinically as efficacious as any other<br>antipsychotic with better tolerability profile."|
|**Section 4**<br>(consideration of the<br>evidence)|"I disagree that risperidone is the most commonly prescribe<br>antipsychotic.<br>We recently completed an audit on prescribing practice in our<br>service, looking at about 200 records. Olanzapine was the most<br>frequently prescribed antipsychotic followed by aripiprazole and<br>qutiepine."|
|**Section 5**<br>(implementation)|In my opinion aripiprazole should be licensed for the younger<br>age group.|
