## **Response to consultee and commentator comments on the draft remit and draft scope (pre-referral)** 

**Please note:** Comments received in the course of consultations carried out by NICE are published in the interests of openness and transparency, and to promote understanding of how recommendations are developed. |**Section**|**Consultee/**<br>**Commentator**|**Comments [sic]**|**Action**|

|Appropriateness|Astrazeneca|Yes. This topic is appropriate for a NICE appraisal.<br>Background:<br><br>Dapagliflozin is an oral anti-diabetic agent; and was the first in the SGLT2<br>inhibitor class to launch in the UK in November 2012. It is licensed for use in<br>adults aged 18 years and older with type 2 diabetes mellitus to improve<br>glycaemic control as monotherapy, add onto metformin, as part of a triple<br>regimen; and as add onto insulin.<br><br>The key trial for dapagliflozin use in a triple regimen was not available at the time<br>of the initial NICE assessment. In the absence of this evidence, dapagliflozin<br>gained a NICE recommendation (TA288) for add onto metformin; and add onto<br>insulin use in July 2013.<br><br>Dapagliflozin triple data was then incorporated into the licence in December<br>2013.<br>UK Real World Evidence (RWE)<br>UK RWE demonstrates that dapagliflozin is already being used in triple regimens<br>(representing approximately one third of dapagliflozin use). This indicates a clinical<br>need for NICE guidance regarding dapagliflozin in triple therapy.|Thank you for your<br>comments.|
