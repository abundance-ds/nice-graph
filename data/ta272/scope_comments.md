||Pierre Fabre|There is currently no recognised therapy for patients with advanced or<br>metastatic TCCU after failure of a platinum-containing regimen. ||RCP|Effective 2ndline chemotherapy in urothelial TCC is certainly an area of<br>unmet clinical need. The vinflunine phase2 data is similar to that of many<br>other agents.
