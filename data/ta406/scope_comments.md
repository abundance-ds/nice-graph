|Innovation|Pfizer||Step-change in disease management<br>There is an unmet need in patients who have ALK+ NSCLC as no targeted<br>therapies are available in the first-line. Crizotinib is innovative as it presents<br>as the first targeted treatment option for these patients.<br>The impact to patients of this innovative targeted therapy is demonstrated<br>through the tumour response data. With standard of care (an untargeted<br>therapy) the majority of patients do not respond to treatment (pemetrexed<br>objective response rate of 45%, Solomon 2014). With crizotinib however,<br>the majority of patients do respond to treatment (objective response rate of<br>74%, Solomon 2014), a result of the drug being a targeted therapy.<br>Furthermore, crizotinib is innovative in that it is an oral therapy which offers<br>an alternative to the standard of care which is intravenously administered.<br>Not only does this ease the disease management for the patient, but it also<br>provides a solution to those patients who have difficulties with intravenous<br>administration.|Comment noted.<br>The company is<br>encouraged to describe<br>the innovative nature of<br>crizotinib in its evidence<br>submission.<br>No action required.|
