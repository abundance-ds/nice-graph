|Background<br>information|British Geriatrics<br>Society|The background section is a useful summary. The<br>National Dementia Strategy was clear about the<br>number of people with dementia. The recent<br>neuropathological data show that most cases of<br>dementia (80% at least, Schneider) show the<br>underlying plaques and tangles associated with a<br>diagnosis of Alzheimer’s disease.
