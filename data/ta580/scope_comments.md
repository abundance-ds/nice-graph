Once a patient’s PSA progresses on dexamethasone, there are<br>currently no available treatment options outside of clinical trials until<br>metastatic disease is seen on imaging.<br>There was a UK wide survey published in abstract form in 2016 on the<br>management of non-metastatic castrate resistant prostate cancer (Latif et al<br>.
