||Roche|Despite introduction of ALK inhibitor therapies, there is a continued and<br>significant unmet need in patients with ALK positive, advanced NSCLC.  J-<br>ALEX is an ongoing phase III study, randomised, head-to-head study<br>comparing two ALK inhibitors (alectinib vs, crizotinib) for the treatment of<br>ALK+ NSCLC.  In February 2016, an independent data monitoring committee<br>recommended that the data from the J-ALEX study are released early after<br>the study met its primary endpoint at a preplanned interim analysis (50% PFS<br>events), demonstrating superiority of alectinib over crizotinib.  Alectinib, and<br>its future indications, offers significant and substantial health-related benefits,<br>and has the potential to be a step change in the management of patients with<br>ALK positive NSCLC.<br>Central nervous system (CNS) metastases are a common site of disease<br>progression in patients with ALK positive, advanced NSCLC.  Development of<br>brain metastases is associated with a reduction in quality of life, and<br>estimated life expectancy.<br>Existing ALK targeted therapies; crizotinib and ceritinib; are substrates for P-<br>gp, which may result in poor CNS penetration.  Preclinical data have shown<br>alectinib is not a substrate for the P-gp efflux transporter, indicating alectinib<br>can penetrate the CNS and elicit clinical activity.  Alectinib phase II data have<br>shown comparable efficacy against systemic disease and CNS metastases.|Thank you for your<br>comments. The<br>appraisal committee will<br>discuss the potential<br>innovative nature of this<br>technology. No changes<br>to the scope needed.|
