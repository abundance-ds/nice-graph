||**NCRI/RCP/RCR**<br>**/ACP/JCCO**|At the time of the first STA on imatinib for the treatment of GIST it was already<br>known that there was a progression-free survival advantage for imaitnib 800<br>mg daily compared with 400 mg, albeit the full paper had not been published.
