|Innovation|Janssen|Ibrutinib is a targeted, non-chemotherapy agent and first-in-class inhibitor of<br>Bruton's tyrosine kinase (BTK). This is a novel therapeutic target and critical<br>signalling kinase in the B cell receptor (BCR) pathway, whose activity is<br>essential for tumour cell survival and proliferation.<br>Regulatory approval in R/R MCL was based on a phase II single arm trial<br>(PCYC1104) due to the high unmet need and unparalleled efficacy with<br>ibrutinib, not observed with any other treatment for R/R MCL. Indeed, ibrutinib<br>was granted US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) breakthrough status in<br>R/R MCL in November 2013. The clinical assessment performed by the<br>CHMP and reported in the European Public Assessment Report (EPAR)<br>states that “the dramatic activity seen in terms of overall response rate (ORR)<br>and DOR is unprecedented historically and considered sufficiently important<br>in this heavily pre-treated patient population to support approval”.<br>Data from the two main ibrutinib clinical trials, PCYC1104 (a phase II single<br>arm study) and RAY (MCL3001, a comparative phase III trial versus<br>temsirolimus), demonstrate that, in patients with R/R MCL, ibrutinib results in<br>an unprecedented benefit in terms of progression free survival (PFS)<br>compared to conventional treatment.<br>Treatment with ibrutinib has been demonstrated to improve quality of life<br>(QOL) as measured by the EuroQol - 5 dimensions (EQ-5D) scale and to also<br>improve symptom control. These improvements are maintained throughout<br>treatment.<br>Anecdotal reports from patients receiving ibrutinib reveal that patients who|Comment noted|
