However, unlike statins, bempedoic acid does not inhibit the<br>cholesterol biosynthesis pathway in skeletal muscle, nor promote the<br>associated cytotoxicity believed to lead to muscle-related side effects. It is<br>differentiated in one key way: bempedoic acid is inactive until it enters the<br>liver where it is converted to its active form by the enzyme, ACSVL1, an<br>enzyme not found in skeletal muscle cells.|Comment noted.
