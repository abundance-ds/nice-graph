|Innovation|Roche|Atezolizumab is a humanised monoclonal antibody immunoglobulin IgG1 that<br>binds directly and selectively to PD-L1 immune checkpoint protein, thus<br>preventing it from binding to receptors PD-1 and B7.1. This prevents down-<br>regulation of T cell activity, allowing for the priming of new T cells to facilitate<br>anticancer immune responses. In parallel, the PD-L2/PD-1 interaction is left<br>intact, potentially preserving peripheral immune homeostasis.<br>It is the first PD-L1 inhibitor to demonstrate efficacy in a treatment-naive, high<br>PD-L1 NSCLC population, with an overall survival hazard ratio of 0.59 versus<br>standard chemotherapy.<br>Atezolizumab monotherapy also offers dosing flexibility, with the option of<br>administration every two, three or four weeks.|Comments noted. The<br>company will have the<br>opportunity to expand<br>on the innovative<br>potential of this<br>technology in its<br>submission and this will<br>be considered by the<br>appraisal committee|
