|Innovation|Eisai Limited|Eisai do consider lenvatinib to be innovative as it is a multiple receptor<br>tyrosine kinase (RTK) inhibitor with a novel binding mode that inhibits the<br>kinase activities of vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) receptors<br>(VEGFR1, VEGFR2 and VEGFR3) and fibroblast growth factor (FGF)<br>receptors (FGFR1, FGFR2, FGFR3 and FGFR4) in addition to other<br>proangiogenic and oncogenic pathway-related RTKs (including the platelet-<br>derived growth factor [PDGF] receptor PDGFRα; KIT; and RET) involved in<br>tumour proliferation.<br>As indicated, sorafenib is currently the only treatment option available in<br>England and Wales for these patients with hepatocellular carcinoma and<br>therefore, there is an unmet need for new treatments which delay progression<br>and improve survival without negatively impacting patient’s quality of life.<br>In the ITT analysis of a large phase III trial , lenvatinib demonstrated non-<br>inferiority in comparison to sorafenib with respect to overall survival (OS). In<br>addition, lenvatinib achieved statistically significant and clinically meaningful<br>improvements in progression-free survival (PFS), time to progression (TTP)<br>and overall response rates (ORR). Importantly, results from the EORTC QLQ-<br>C30 show that there was no decrease in health related quality of life (HRQoL)<br>for those patients treated with lenvatinib.|Thank you for your<br>comments, no changes<br>are needed.|
